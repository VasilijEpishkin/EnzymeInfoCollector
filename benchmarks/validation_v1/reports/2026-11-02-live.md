## Regression found and fixed

Before the fix, 19 of 20 accessions succeeded; valid TrEMBL
A0A024QG36 was rejected by the shared UniProt accession regex.
The former third alternative was an invalid 11-character pattern. Replaced
with the official 6-/10-character pattern from
https://www.uniprot.org/help/accession_numbers and added offline
positive/negative tests. After the fix, the TrEMBL entry is accepted
and matches the observed direct RHEA:20309 master annotation.
